# Wiring differences between a generated panel and the drawing in the note it answers that are
# ACCEPTED, one per line, `<selector> | <the difference, as scripts/svg-check reports it>`.
# `./scripts/cd-check` fails unless a panel's differences are EXACTLY these, so a line deleted here
# is a difference nobody has agreed to and turns the gate red just as a new one does.
#
# A difference is accepted only when the note's drawing says something the STATEMENT does not, or
# lays out corners of its own.  A wrong direction, an arrow through a label, a chord that is not
# straight — none of those can be accepted: they are the drawer's bug, and the drawer is fixed.
#
# COLOUR is the one kind of difference that may be accepted here, and only for `<banana-split>`.
# The note colours that picture BY COMPONENT — `⦇h⦈`, `π₁`, `A` in GIVEN1 and `⦇k⦈`, `π₂`, `B` in
# GIVEN2, so a reader follows one product component down each side, with `A×B` INDUCED and `T`
# black.  The rule the generator applies reads the ROLE off the statement instead: `⦇h⦈` and `⦇k⦈`
# are both arrows the statement is handed (GIVEN1), `π₁` and `π₂` both the structure the universal
# property is about (GIVEN2), `T` an end of GIVEN1 arrows and of no GIVEN2 one, and `A`, `B`, `A×B`
# ends of a GIVEN2 arrow, hence black.  The eight lines below are exactly that disagreement; the
# user has been told of it and will decide which of the two the note keeps.  Wiring is never
# accepted here — every panel with a reference drawing joins up exactly as the note draws it.
Freyd.Alg.relCata_pair_beta | panel 1 adds the GIVEN1 arrow from column 1 row 0 to column 2 row 1 that cdref-banana.svg has not
Freyd.Alg.relCata_pair_beta | panel 1 adds the GIVEN2 arrow from column 1 row 1 to column 0 row 1 that cdref-banana.svg has not

Freyd.Alg.relCata_pair_beta | panel 1 draws the node at column 1 row 0 in GIVEN1, where cdref-banana.svg draws it in black
Freyd.Alg.relCata_pair_beta | panel 1 draws the node at column 1 row 1 in black, where cdref-banana.svg draws it in INDUCED
Freyd.Alg.relCata_pair_beta | panel 1 draws the node at column 2 row 1 in black, where cdref-banana.svg draws it in GIVEN2
